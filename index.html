The Intelligence Explosion
The curve goes vertical.
Why the next decade will not feel like the last one, and why that is not hype.
01 · The trend
Every input is compounding
Compute, algorithms and investment all grow at once. Straight-line forecasts keep losing to the curve.
02 · The mechanism
AI starts building AI
Smarter models
Better at code, math and research.
Automated research
Models run experiments and write the next model.
Faster progress
Better chips, data and algorithms arrive sooner.
Smarter again
Every lap is shorter than the last.
02 · What compresses
Years of work become days
Software
A team’s quarter becomes an afternoon of agents writing, testing and shipping.
Science
Literature review, hypotheses and experiments run in parallel, around the clock.
AI itself
Models help design chips, training runs and their own successors.
Economy
Any work done on a screen is in play. Capability moves in months, adaptation in years.
03 · The old idea
The last invention we need to make.
I. J. Good argued in 1965 that a machine smarter than any human would design even better machines, and we would be left behind. It was a thought experiment. Now it is a roadmap debate.
04 · What to do
Move at the speed of the curve
Verify
We cannot trust what we cannot test. Invest in evaluation and interpretability before capability outruns them.
Secure and align
Protect model weights, build safeguards, and treat misuse and misalignment as engineering problems.
Adapt fast
Update institutions, skills and policy on a timeline of months. Plan for abundance and for shocks.
05 · Where this leaves us
Strap in.
The only clearly wrong move is planning for a slow decade.
